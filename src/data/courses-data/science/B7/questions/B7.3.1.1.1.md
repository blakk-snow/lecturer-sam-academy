# Materials and their properties practice

### Q1
type: mcq
question: Which of these is a property of a solid?
- [ ] It takes the shape of its container
- [ ] It can be poured easily
- [x] It keeps its own shape
- [ ] It spreads to fill all available space
explanation: A solid keeps its own shape because its particles are tightly packed together.
difficulty: easy

### Q2
type: mcq
question: When water is heated to its boiling point, it changes to
- [ ] ice
- [x] steam
- [ ] salt
- [ ] syrup
explanation: Heating water to 100 °C changes it into water vapour (steam) — an example of evaporation/boiling.
difficulty: easy

### Q3
type: trueFalse
question: Melting is the change from a solid to a liquid.
- [x] True
- [ ] False
explanation: Melting happens when a solid is heated and changes into a liquid, like ice becoming water.
difficulty: easy

### Q4
type: fillBlank
question: The change from a gas to a liquid is called ____
answer: condensation
accepted: condensing
explanation: Condensation is when a gas cools and changes into a liquid, like water droplets forming on a cold bottle.
difficulty: medium
