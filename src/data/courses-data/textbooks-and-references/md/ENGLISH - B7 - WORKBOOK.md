### How to use this workbook

- **Work in pencil first.** Then you can correct without spoiling the page.

- **Do the Recall section before you open the Learner's Book.** It shows what you still remember from the chapter before.

- **Plan before you write.** The planning frame is not optional: examiners can tell at once who planned.

- **Two sections cannot be marked from the page.** The speaking and listening task must be performed aloud and signed, and the portfolio piece is collected over several weeks.

- **Read your writing aloud before you hand it in.** Your ear catches what your eye misses.

- **Fill in the Review box at the end of every chapter.** The two lines about what you got wrong are the most useful lines in the book.

> **Guided practice — Taking notes while listening**

*Follow the working. Fill in the missing steps.* Do not try to write every word. Use the six questions. **Who?** the speaker and anyone they name. **What?** the event in a phrase. **When and where? How many?** — every ______ , exactly as given. **Why?** the reason given. **So what?** the consequence or the action asked for. Leave a wide margin on the right for your own ______ , so that fact and reaction never mix. **The rule about numbers.** Write a figure the instant you hear it, even in the middle of a word. A sentence can be rebuilt from memory; a number cannot.

> **Guided practice — Telling fact from opinion**

*Follow the working. Fill in the missing steps.*

**Fact:** *Uptake last year reached sixty-one per cent.* It can be checked.

**Opinion:** *That is a failure of planning.* Others may reasonably disagree. Mark F or O: *The clinic has two nurses.* ______ Mark F or O: *The clinic is badly run.* ______ **Signalling your own opinion.** Begin with *In my view…* or *It seems to me…* so that nobody has to guess which part is fact.

## Things to know by heart

*Test yourself on these once a week.*

#### Punctuation

| **Mark** | **Use it for** |
| --- | --- |
| Full stop | the end of a statement |
| Question mark | the end of a question |
| Comma | a list, or an interruption inside a sentence |
| Apostrophe | possession (the boy’s bag) and contraction (don’t) |
| Quotation marks | a speaker’s exact words |
| Capital letter | a sentence opening, a proper noun, and the pronoun I |

#### Letters

| **Salutation** | **Subscription** |
| --- | --- |
| Dear Sir / Dear Madam | Yours faithfully |
| Dear Mr Asare / Dear Madam Rose | Yours sincerely |
| Dear Ama (a friend) | Yours sincerely, or Your friend |

#### The writing process

| **Stage** | **What you do** |
| --- | --- |
| Planning | decide subject, purpose and reader; gather points |
| Drafting | write it through without stopping to correct |
| Revising | change what it says and the order |
| Editing | correct grammar, spelling and punctuation |
| Publishing | write the clean final copy |
