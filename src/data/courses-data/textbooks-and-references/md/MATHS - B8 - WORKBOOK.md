### How to use this workbook

- **Work in pencil first.** Then you can correct without spoiling the page.

- **Do the Recall section before you open the Learner's Book.** It shows you what you still remember from last year and from the chapter before.

- **Everybody does the Core section.** Attempt the Challenge section only when the Core section is complete and correct.

- **Show all your working in the boxes.** In an examination most of the marks are for method, not for the final answer.

- **Mark your own work honestly.** A tick you did not earn costs you in the examination, not now.

- **Fill in the Review box at the end of every chapter.** The two lines about what you got wrong are the most useful lines in the book.

#### Other facts

- Angles on a straight line add to 180°; angles at a point add to 360°.

- The angles of a triangle add to 180°.

- Complementary angles add to 90°; supplementary angles add to 180°.

\- 1 km = 1,000 m; 1 m = 100 cm; 1 cm = 10 mm.

- Take π as 22/7 unless the question says otherwise.

- A bearing is measured clockwise from north and written with three figures.

- Any number raised to the power zero is 1.

- All probabilities of an experiment add up to 1.
