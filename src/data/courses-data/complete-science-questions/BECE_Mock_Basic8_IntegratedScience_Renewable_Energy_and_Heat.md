- **What it is** - Heat: a **form of energy that flows from a hotter body to a cooler one**; Temperature: the **degree of hotness or coldness of a body** (2 marks).
- **SI unit** - Heat: the **joule (J)**; Temperature: the **kelvin (K)** (commonly measured in degrees Celsius) (2 marks).
- **How it is measured** - Heat: with a **calorimeter** (or found from the mass and the temperature change); Temperature: with a **thermometer** (2 marks).
  - *Marking note: 6 marks, 1 mark for each correct entry in the table. Accept 'degree Celsius' for the unit of temperature, but the kelvin is the SI unit. This table answers the curriculum exemplar on the distinguishing features of heat and temperature.*

**(b)** [2 marks]

- **When heat is supplied to a body its temperature rises; when heat is taken away the temperature falls** (1 mark).
- For the same substance, the **greater the heat supplied the greater the rise in temperature**, though the rise also depends on the **mass of the body** (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each.*

**(c)** [1 marks]

- From the **body at the higher temperature to the body at the lower temperature** (from hotter to cooler)
  - *Marking note: 1 mark.*

**(d)** [1 marks]

- **27 + 273 = 300 K**
  - *Marking note: 1 mark for 300 K. Accept the working 27 + 273 shown with the correct answer; the unit K should be written.*

### Question 5 (9 marks)

**(a)** [1 marks]

- **Cup A**, whose thermometer reads 90 °C
  - *Marking note: 1 mark.*

**(b)** [3 marks]

- **Bucket B** (1 mark).
- Although its **temperature is lower**, it holds a **far greater mass of water - 2 000 cm³ against 100 cm³** (1 mark).
- The **heat energy in a body depends on its mass as well as on its temperature**, so the total heat energy stored in B is greater (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 3 marks: 1 mark for naming B and 2 marks for the reason. Naming B with no reason earns 1 mark only.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

- **Temperature measures only the degree of hotness** of the water and **does not depend on how much water there is** (1 mark).
- **Heat is the total energy in the water and depends on the mass as well as on the temperature**, so the hotter container is not necessarily the one with more heat energy (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. This is the key distinction in the standard.*

**(d)** [3 marks]

- Its **temperature falls** (1 mark),
- because the water is **hotter than its surroundings, so heat flows out of it into the cup and the surrounding air** (1 mark),
- and it goes on losing heat **until the water and the surroundings are at the same temperature**, that is room temperature (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 3 marks, 1 mark each. A candidate who writes that 'coldness enters the water' loses the second mark; heat flows out, cold does not flow in.*

### Question 6 (10 marks)

**(a)** [3 marks]

- A **beaker (or small saucepan) containing water**
- A **thermometer**
- A **source of heat** (Bunsen burner, spirit lamp or stove), with a **tripod stand and wire gauze**, and a **clock or stop-watch**
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any THREE suitable items (3 marks). The thermometer and the heat source should appear.*

**(b)** [4 marks]

- **Pour a measured volume of water into the beaker** and stand it on the gauze on the tripod.
- **Place the thermometer in the water** with its bulb well under the surface but **not touching the bottom**, and **read the starting temperature**.
- **Light the burner and start the stop-watch at the same moment.**
- **Read and record the temperature every one minute**, stirring gently, until the water boils; then **switch off the heat and compare the readings**.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any FOUR correct steps in a workable order (4 marks). The correct placing of the thermometer bulb and the regular timed readings should appear for full marks.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

- A **ruled table with the headings 'Time (minutes)' and 'Temperature of the water (°C)'** (1 mark).
- **Units shown in the headings and blank rows provided** for the readings (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks as shown. A table without units in the headings scores 1 mark.*

**(d)** [1 marks]

- **Handle the hot beaker with a cloth or tongs and keep away from the flame and the boiling water** (accept: do not stir with the thermometer; tie back loose clothing; stand clear of the steam).
  - *Marking note: 1 mark for any one sound precaution. 'Be careful' on its own earns no mark.*
