  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. Award 1 mark for ‘food given to farm animals’ alone. Do NOT accept ‘grass’ by itself.*

**(b)** [4 marks]

- (i) Cattle - **grass, forage, hay or silage**
- (ii) Laying hen - **layer mash**
- (iii) Pig - **pig grower mash or pellets** (accept kitchen and farm waste)
- (iv) Day-old chick - **chick mash (starter mash)**
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each (4 marks). The feed must suit the animal AND its age. Do NOT accept ‘layer mash’ for a day-old chick or ‘silage’ for a chick.*

**(c)** [3 marks]

- **Proteins**
- **Carbohydrates**
- **Fats and oils**
- **Mineral salts**
- **Vitamins** (also accept **water** and **roughage/fibre**)
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any THREE correct classes (3 marks). Do NOT accept named feedstuffs such as ‘maize’ or ‘fish meal’ here - those are sources, not classes of nutrients.*

### Question 2 (11 marks)

**(a)** [1 marks]

- **Carbohydrate** (55 %)
  - *Marking note: 1 mark. Accept ‘carbohydrate’ with or without the percentage.*

**(b)** [2 marks]

- Protein = 22 % of 50 kg = (22 ÷ 100) x 50 (1 mark)
- = **11 kg** (1 mark)
  - *Marking note: 2 marks: 1 method mark for the correct working and 1 mark for the answer with the unit. Award the method mark even if the arithmetic is wrong. A bare correct answer of 11 kg scores both marks.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

- **Chicks are growing rapidly**, and protein is the nutrient that **builds body tissues, muscles and feathers** (1 mark).
- **Adult birds have finished growing**, so they need mainly energy and maintenance feed and need a smaller proportion of protein (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. The first mark is for linking protein to growth or body building; the second is for the comparison with adult birds.*

**(d)** [2 marks]

- To check that the **proportions of nutrients suit the type and age** of his animals (for example starter mash for chicks).
- To **compare the quality and price** of different brands, and to check the **net mass** and the **expiry (or manufacturing) date** so that stale feed is not bought.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO sound reasons (2 marks). This is the curriculum exemplar on selecting feed from the proportions shown on the label.*

**(e)** [2 marks]

- **Mineral salts** - calcium and phosphorus (1 mark)
- used by a laying hen to form **strong egg shells** (also accept **strong bones**) (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. Accept ‘calcium’ for the nutrient mark. The egg-shell answer is the expected use for a laying hen.*

**(f)** [2 marks]

- To stop the feed from **taking up moisture and going mouldy** - mouldy feed can **poison the animals**.
- To **keep out rats, weevils and other pests**, and to **preserve the nutrients** so that the feed stays fresh until it is used.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct reasons (2 marks).*

### Question 3 (10 marks)

**(a)** [4 marks]

- (ii) Carbohydrate - **supplies energy** for movement, work and warmth

- (iv) Vitamins - **protect the animal from diseases** and keep the body working properly
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each (4 marks). Accept any one correct use for each nutrient. Do NOT accept ‘it makes the animal strong’ for more than one of them.*

**(d)** [2 marks]

**(e)** [2 marks]

- They would **stop growing and begin to lose mass**, stop feeding and become very weak, with sunken eyes and dry skin (1 mark).
- Within a few days the chicks would **die** (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. This is the curriculum exemplar on predicting what happens to animals not provided with adequate water; death must appear for the second mark.*

**(f)** [2 marks]

- **Water is necessary for the proper growth of animals** (1 mark).
- Animals given **enough clean water grow much faster** than those given too little, **even when they are given the same feed** (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. The conclusion must refer to water AND to growth. Do NOT accept ‘Group P is better than Group Q’, which merely restates the result.*

**(g)** [1 marks]

- The **type and quantity of feed** given to both groups (also accept the **breed, age and number of chicks** or the **housing and temperature**).
  - *Marking note: 1 mark. Do NOT award a mark for ‘the amount of water’ - that is the factor being changed.*

### Question 5 (9 marks)

**(a)** [4 marks]

- It **helps in the digestion** of food and softens the feed in the gut.
- It **dissolves nutrients and carries them round the body** in the blood.
- It helps to **control the body temperature** of the animal through sweating and panting.
- It forms a large part of the **blood, milk and body fluids**, and helps in **removing waste** from the body as urine.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any FOUR correct uses (4 marks). Do NOT award two marks for the same idea stated twice in different words.*

**(b)** [3 marks]

- **Dry nose, muzzle and mouth**, with **sunken eyes** and a dull, rough coat.
- **Loss of appetite and loss of body mass**, with weakness and slow movement.
- **Little urine passed, and it is dark and strong-smelling**; the animals crowd round the trough whenever water is brought.
- A **fall in the production of milk or eggs**.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any THREE correct signs (3 marks). Accept any observable sign of dehydration.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

- **Wash the drinking troughs daily** and **change the water regularly** (at least twice a day).
- **Place the troughs where droppings, dust and litter cannot fall in** (raise them slightly), and **cover the water storage tank**.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO practical ways (2 marks).*

### Question 6 (9 marks)

**(a)** [4 marks]

- **Give the right feed for the age of the birds** (starter mash first, then grower and finisher) at **fixed times each day**.
- **Measure out the correct quantity** of feed for the fifty birds, so that there is neither shortage nor waste.
- **Wash the drinkers and feeders** and **provide clean, fresh water at all times**, changing it at least twice a day.
- **Remove stale, wet or mouldy feed** and soiled litter, and **observe the birds**, reporting any that look sick or are not eating.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any FOUR sensible daily practices (4 marks). This is a practical design task, so award method marks. Water must be mentioned somewhere in the answer for full marks.*

**(b)** [2 marks]

- The **quantity of feed used** each day or week and the **money spent** on it.
- The **body mass of the birds each week** (to check their growth), and the **number of birds that are alive, sick or dead**.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO useful records (2 marks). Accept a record of sales or of vaccination dates.*

**(c)** [3 marks]

- Keep the bags in a **cool, dry, well-ventilated room** so that the feed does not absorb moisture and go mouldy.
- **Stack the bags on pallets or planks off the floor** and away from the wall, and **keep rats and insects out** of the store.
- **Use the oldest bags first** and check the expiry date; **never store feed together with insecticides, fertilisers or other chemicals**.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any THREE correct precautions (3 marks). Accept any sound storage practice that protects the feed from damp, pests or contamination.*

### EXAMINER NOTES

- This sub-strand carries TWO content standards. Questions 1 to 3 and the Section A items on feed and nutrients assess B8/JHS2.2.4.1; Questions 4 and 5 and the water items assess B8/JHS2.2.4.2. Question 6 draws on both. The blueprint shows which standard each item belongs to.
