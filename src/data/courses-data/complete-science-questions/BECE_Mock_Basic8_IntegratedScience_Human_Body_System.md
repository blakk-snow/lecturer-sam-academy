  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. The first mark is for locating the nerves in the pulp; the second is for the exposure or irritation of those nerves.*

### Question 4 (10 marks)

**(a)** [2 marks]

- Tooth decay is the **gradual breaking down or destruction of the hard parts of a tooth** (enamel and dentine) (1 mark)
- by the **acids produced by bacteria** acting on food left on the teeth, so that **holes (cavities) are formed** (1 mark).

- The **bacteria in the plaque feed on the sugar and produce acid** (1 mark).
- The **acid dissolves the enamel and then the dentine**, forming a hole (cavity) which may finally reach the pulp cavity and cause toothache (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 3 marks, 1 mark each, and the steps must be in a sensible order. The words plaque and acid should both appear; award the marks for the correct science even where the term plaque is missing.*

**(a)** [4 marks]

- **Brush the teeth properly at least twice a day**, especially last thing before going to bed.
- **Reduce the eating of sweets, toffees and sugary drinks**, particularly between meals, and **rinse the mouth with clean water after eating**.
- **Eat foods rich in calcium** (milk, small fish eaten with the bones) and plenty of **fresh fruits and vegetables**.
- **Visit the dentist regularly** (at least twice a year) for check-ups, and **avoid using the teeth to open bottle tops** or crack hard objects.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any FOUR correct ways (4 marks). Do NOT accept ‘take medicine’ or ‘remove the tooth’ - these are treatments, not prevention.*

**(b)** [3 marks]

- **Wet the brush and put a little toothpaste on it** (or chew the end of the chewing stick until it is soft and brush-like) (1 mark).
- **Brush all the surfaces** - the outer, the inner and the biting surfaces - using **short up-and-down (and circular) strokes, brushing away from the gum**, not only across the front teeth (1 mark).
- **Brush for about two minutes**, also **clean the tongue**, then **rinse the mouth with clean water** and rinse the brush; replace the brush when the bristles spread (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 3 marks, 1 mark each. This is the ‘demonstrate proper ways of cleaning the teeth’ exemplar, so award method marks for a sound procedure. The idea of reaching ALL surfaces must appear for full marks.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

- **Milk** (and other dairy foods such as cheese and yoghurt)
- **Small fishes eaten with their bones**, eggs, beans, green leafy vegetables such as kontomire, and fresh fruits
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct foods (2 marks). The food should be a source of calcium, vitamins or phosphorus. Do NOT accept sweets or soft drinks.*

### Question 6 (10 marks)

**(a)** [4 marks]

- **‘Brush your teeth twice every day - and never miss bedtime.’**
- **‘Cut down on toffees and fizzy drinks; rinse your mouth after eating.’**
- **‘Eat milk, small fish and fruits for strong teeth.’**
- **‘Visit the dentist twice a year; never use your teeth to open bottles.’** (with a clear drawing of a tooth or a brushing action)
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any FOUR sensible points (4 marks). This is a design task; award method marks for any message that would really help a Basic 4 pupil care for the teeth. A drawing or slogan may earn one of the marks.*

**(b)** [2 marks]

- A **toothbrush with toothpaste**
- A **chewing stick (sokodua)**; also accept **salt with clean water** or dental floss
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO materials genuinely available locally (2 marks). The chewing stick is widely used in Ghana and must be accepted.*

**(c)** [3 marks]

- **Put a clean egg shell into a cup containing the fizzy drink**, and an identical shell into a cup of **clean water as a control** (1 mark).
- **Leave both cups for a day or two**, then remove the shells and compare them (1 mark).
- The shell from the fizzy drink is **soft, rough, stained and partly dissolved**, while the one from the water is unchanged - showing that the **acid in the drink attacks the hard material of the tooth (the enamel)** (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 3 marks, 1 mark each. This is a design task, so award method marks for any workable procedure. The control (shell in water) earns a mark, and the conclusion must link the change to acid acting on the enamel. The egg shell is used because it is made of a hard mineral material like enamel.*

**(d)** [1 marks]

- **Do not allow the pupils to drink the liquid or taste the egg shells**, and **wash the hands with soap** after handling them (keep the cups covered and labelled).
  - *Marking note: 1 mark for any one sensible precaution. Do NOT accept ‘be careful’ on its own.*
