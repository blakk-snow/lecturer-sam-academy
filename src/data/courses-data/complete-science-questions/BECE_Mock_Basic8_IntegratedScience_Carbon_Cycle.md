### Question 1 (8 marks)

**(a)** [2 marks]

- The carbon cycle is the **continuous movement (circulation) of carbon** between the air, living things, the soil and **back to the air again**.
- Carbon passes from carbon (IV) oxide in the air into green plants, then to animals, and back into the air, repeatedly.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct points (2 marks). Award 1 mark for ‘carbon moving round and round’ if the idea of movement between air and living things is present. Do NOT accept ‘the cycle of carbon’ alone.*

**(b)** [4 marks]

- **Photosynthesis**
- **Respiration**
- **Burning** (combustion)
- **Decay** (decomposition)
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for all FOUR processes (4 marks). Accept ‘combustion’ for burning and ‘decomposition’ for decay. Do NOT accept ‘eating’ or ‘food chain’ as one of the four processes.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

- The **same carbon atoms are used again and again** - carbon is not destroyed or used up at any stage.
- The **processes repeat themselves over and over** in nature (carbon returns to the air and is taken up again).
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct reasons (2 marks). Accept ‘it goes round and round’ only when the idea of repetition or of reuse is stated.*

- It uses **oxygen** and gives out **carbon (IV) oxide** (and water) - it happens in all living cells, day and night.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct points (2 marks). Award 1 mark for ‘getting energy from food’ on its own; do NOT accept ‘breathing’ without the idea of energy from food.*

**(b)** [4 marks]

- Photosynthesis **takes in carbon (IV) oxide** and gives out oxygen; respiration **takes in oxygen** and gives out carbon (IV) oxide.
- Photosynthesis **needs light** and takes place only in **green plants**; respiration takes place in **all living things**, in light or in darkness, day and night.
- Photosynthesis **stores energy** in food; respiration **releases energy** from food.
  - *Marking note: 2 marks each for any TWO correct differences (4 marks): 1 mark for each half of the comparison. Do NOT award a mark for a difference that describes only one process without contrasting it with the other.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct fuels (2 marks). Accept any fuel really burnt in Ghana.*

**(d)** [1 marks]

  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. Accept ‘the snake eats the rat, so it takes in the carbon that was in the rat’ for full marks if the idea of eating is clear.*

**(d)** [2 marks]

- **Bacteria and fungi (decomposers)** break down the body of the dead snake (1 mark).
- Carbon (IV) oxide is **given out during the decay** and released into the air, so the carbon returns to the air (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. The two ideas required are the decomposers doing the breaking down and the carbon returning to the air as carbon (IV) oxide.*

### Question 7 (10 marks)

**(a)** [2 marks]

- The greenhouse effect is the **trapping (holding) of heat** from the sun by gases in the air.
- This heat is kept near the **earth's surface**, so the earth stays warm enough for life.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct points (2 marks). Award 1 mark for ‘gases trap heat’ on its own; do NOT accept ‘the earth is hot’.*

**(b)** [2 marks]

- Too much carbon (IV) oxide **traps too much heat** in the atmosphere (1 mark).
- The earth therefore becomes **warmer than normal (global warming)**, which changes the climate (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. The chain of reasoning (more gas - more heat trapped - earth warms) must be present for both marks.*

**(c)** [4 marks]

- Green plants **remove carbon (IV) oxide** from the air and **give out oxygen** during photosynthesis (1 mark).
- Respiration in living things, burning and decay **add carbon (IV) oxide** to the air and use up oxygen (1 mark).
- Because the two sets of processes **go on at the same time**, the gases that are removed are replaced (1 mark).
- This keeps the amounts of carbon (IV) oxide and oxygen in the air **fairly constant (balanced)**, which keeps life on earth going (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 4 marks, 1 mark each. Award marks for the pairs of processes named and for the idea of balance. A candidate who names only one side of the cycle earns a maximum of 2 marks.*

**(d)** [2 marks]

- **Suggestion:** planting trees and keeping them on the farm (agroforestry), or using an improved (LPG) cooker instead of firewood, or **composting crop waste instead of burning it** (1 mark).
- **Benefit:** the trees remove carbon (IV) oxide from the air and give out oxygen - and they also give shade, fruit, fodder, firewood or extra income (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks: 1 mark for a sensible suggestion, 1 mark for a real benefit. This is a design task, so award method marks for any practical suggestion that would genuinely reduce carbon (IV) oxide in the air.*
