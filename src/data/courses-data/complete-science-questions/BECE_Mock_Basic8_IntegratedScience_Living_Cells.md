### Question 4 (10 marks)

**(a)** [3 marks]

- V - **nucleus**
- VI - **chloroplast**
- VII - **mitochondrion**
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each (3 marks). Do NOT accept ‘cell’ for V or ‘vacuole’ for VII.*

**(b)** [1 marks]

- The chloroplast **traps light energy** (through chlorophyll) and uses it to **make food** during photosynthesis.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark for either point, or for ‘it makes food for the plant by photosynthesis’.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

- It has a **true nucleus** enclosed by a **nuclear membrane** (1 mark).
- It has **membrane-bound organelles** such as mitochondria, chloroplasts and a vacuole (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. Accept ‘it has a nucleus’ for the first mark only if the enclosed/true nucleus idea is clear.*

**(d)** [2 marks]

- Plants **make their own food** by photosynthesis, and the chloroplast (with chlorophyll) is needed to **trap light energy** (1 mark).
- Animals **feed on ready-made food**, so they have no need for chloroplasts (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. The answer must contrast how plants and animals obtain food.*

**(e)** [2 marks]

- **Water enters the cell** (through the partially permeable membrane) and the cell becomes **firm and swollen (turgid)** (1 mark).
- Explanation: water moves from where it is in plenty into the cell, and the **strong cell wall prevents the cell from bursting** (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. Accept ‘the vacuole fills up with water and pushes on the cell wall’ as the explanation. Do NOT accept ‘the cell bursts’ for a plant cell.*

**(a)** [4 marks]

- A **eukaryotic cell** has a **true nucleus enclosed by a nuclear membrane** (1 mark) and **membrane-bound organelles** such as mitochondria and chloroplasts (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 4 marks, 1 mark for each of the four ideas. Accept ‘a bacterium is prokaryotic’ for one mark only if the structural idea is given as well.*

**(b)** [6 marks]

- **Prokaryotic cell:** a bacterium; a blue-green alga.
- **Eukaryotic cell:** Amoeba; a mushroom; a human cheek cell; a maize leaf cell.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark for each organism placed in the correct column (6 marks). The columns must be correct: a bacterium and a blue-green alga are the ONLY prokaryotic organisms listed. Do NOT award a mark for an organism placed in the wrong column.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

- A prokaryotic cell has **no true nucleus**; a eukaryotic cell has a **true nucleus**.
- A prokaryotic cell has **no membrane-bound organelles**; a eukaryotic cell has **membrane-bound organelles**.
- Prokaryotes are usually **unicellular** small cells; eukaryotes may be unicellular or multicellular and are larger.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct differences (2 marks). The difference must be stated as a comparison.*

- **Cook food well** and wash fruits and vegetables before eating them.
- **Visit the hospital** for treatment when you are sick so that you do not spread the disease.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any THREE sensible measures (3 marks). The measure must really prevent the spread of bacterial disease. Do NOT accept ‘take medicine every day’ without a reason, or ‘stay away from people’ on its own.*

**(c)** [1 marks]

- Bacteria are useful in **fermenting milk into yoghurt** (also accept: fermenting maize dough for kenkey; making cheese; producing some medicines; helping to decompose waste to form manure).
  - *Marking note: 1 mark for any one correct use. Do NOT accept ‘they are used for food’ on its own.*

### Question 7 (6 marks)

**(a)** [2 marks]

- **Prokaryotic organisms** - made up of cells with **no true nucleus**.
- **Eukaryotic organisms** - made up of cells with a **true nucleus**.
  - *Marking note: 2 marks: 1 mark for naming the two groups and 1 mark for a correct description of at least one of them. Accept ‘prokaryotes and eukaryotes’.*

**(b)** [2 marks]

- **Observe and list** the organisms (or pictures of them) to be studied.
- Look at the **type of cell** each organism is made of (with a microscope or from charts), **record the results in a table**, and place each organism in the correct group.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO sensible steps (2 marks). Look for observing, recording in a table, and using the cell type as the criterion.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

- A plant cell has a **cell wall**, **cell membrane** and **cytoplasm**.
- It also has a **nucleus**, **chloroplasts** and a **large central vacuole**.
  - *Marking note: 2 marks: 1 mark for any three correctly named parts, 1 mark for the remaining parts. Do NOT award a mark for parts that belong to animal cells only.*
