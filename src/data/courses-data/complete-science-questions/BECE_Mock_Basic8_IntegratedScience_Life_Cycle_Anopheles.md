### Question 1 (8 marks)

**(a)** [2 marks]

- A vector is an **organism (insect or other animal) that carries a disease-causing organism** from one host to another.
- The vector itself may not become sick; it only carries the germ to the person or animal it bites.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct points (2 marks). Award 1 mark for ‘an insect that carries disease’ if it is not developed further. Do NOT accept ‘an animal that causes disease’ on its own - the vector carries the germ, it does not make the germ.*

**(b)** [4 marks]

- **Egg**
- **Larva** (wriggler)
- **Pupa**
- **Adult** (imago)
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for all FOUR stages in the correct order (4 marks). The order must be correct for full marks; an order that puts the pupa before the larva earns a maximum of 2 marks and a comment.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

- **Water:** the egg, the larva and the pupa
- **Land/air:** the adult mosquito
  - *Marking note: 1 mark for naming the water stages and 1 mark for the adult stage (2 marks). Accept ‘the young stages (egg, larva, pupa) live in water’.*

### Question 2 (9 marks)

**(a)** [4 marks]

- (i) **eggs** (laid on the surface of water)
- (ii) **larva** (wriggler)
- (iii) **pupa**
- (iv) **adult mosquito**
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each (4 marks). The stage must be matched to the correct number. Accept ‘wriggler’ for (ii) and ‘imago’ for (iv).*

**(b)** [1 marks]

- **Complete metamorphosis**
  - *Marking note: 1 mark. Do NOT accept ‘incomplete metamorphosis’ or ‘metamorphosis’ alone.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

- The **larva lives in water** and has **no wings**; the **adult lives on land** and **has wings**.
- The larva **breathes through a tube** at the water surface and feeds on tiny water plants or animals; the adult feeds on **nectar (male) or blood (female)**.
- The larva **crawls (wriggles)** while the adult **flies**.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct differences (2 marks). The difference must be a comparison between (ii) and (iv); a statement about only one of them earns no mark.*

**(d)** [2 marks]

- The **young stages (egg, larva and pupa) can only develop in water** - they need water to live (1 mark).
- The larva and pupa **breathe air at the surface** of the water, so the eggs must be laid where there is water (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. Accept ‘the young ones cannot live outside water’ for the first mark and ‘they need water to breathe and grow’ for the second.*

### Question 3 (10 marks)

**(a)** [4 marks]

- I - **proboscis**
- II - **antenna** (antennae)
- III - **wings**
- IV - **abdomen**
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each (4 marks). The label must be matched to the correct part. Do NOT accept ‘nose’ for I or ‘tail’ for IV.*

**(b)** [2 marks]

- The proboscis **pierces the skin** and is used for **sucking blood** (1 mark).
- In the female Anopheles it also **passes the plasmodium (the malaria germ) into the blood** of the person she bites (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. Award the first mark for piercing/sucking; the second mark is for the link with malaria. Accept ‘it is the mouth part of the mosquito’ only with piercing or sucking.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

- Only the **female bites human beings and takes blood** (1 mark).
- She needs the blood for the **development of her eggs**, and the **plasmodium in her body is passed into the person's blood** when she bites (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. Do NOT award a mark for ‘the male has no proboscis’ - that statement is wrong.*

**(d)** [1 marks]

- It rests with its **body at an angle (sloping) to the surface**, not flat on it.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark. The key idea is the angled (tilted) resting posture; accept a correct drawing that shows the tilted body.*

**(e)** [1 marks]

- The **adult has wings and flies (lives on land)**; the larva has **no wings, lives in water and crawls**.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark for any one correct difference. Do NOT accept ‘the adult is bigger’ on its own.*

### Question 4 (12 marks)

**(a)** [2 marks]

- The **economic importance** of an organism is the way it **affects people's money, work and wealth** (whether it helps or harms them).
- It is the **value** of the organism to humans: what it costs or gives in money, work and health, and to farming and industry.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct points (2 marks). Award 1 mark for ‘how it is useful or harmful to people’. Do NOT accept ‘how many there are’.*

**(b)** [2 marks]

- Mosquito **larvae and adults are food** for fishes, birds, bats and other animals in the **food chain**.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark for each correct point, maximum 2 marks. Accept ‘they are part of the food chain’ with an example. Do NOT accept ‘they make honey’ or ‘they add nutrients to the soil’.*

**(c)** [3 marks]

- They **spread malaria** (the female Anopheles carries the plasmodium).
- Their **bites are painful and disturb sleep**, and they are a **nuisance** at home.
- Time and money are **lost through sickness and treatment** - people miss work, school and farming.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any THREE correct points (3 marks). Do NOT accept ‘they are bad’ or ‘they bite’ without a stated harm.*

**(d)** [3 marks]

- The pupil **stays away from school** while sick, so lessons are **missed** (1 mark).
- Weakness and fever make **concentration hard**, so performance in class and in examinations **falls** (1 mark).
- Money is spent on **treatment and medicine** instead of on school needs such as books or fees; repeated sickness may even make the pupil **drop out** (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 3 marks, 1 mark each for any three sound points. Look for the loss of lessons, the effect on performance, and the cost or drop-out risk.*

**(e)** [2 marks]

  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO correct points (2 marks). The answer must name an effect on work, production or money.*

### Question 5 (10 marks)

**(a)** [2 marks]

- The stages found in water are the **egg, the larva and the pupa** (1 mark).
- They are **concentrated in the water** and **cannot fly away**, so they can be killed or poured away easily - unlike the adult mosquitoes that fly about (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. Accept ‘the young stages live in water’ for the first mark.*

**(b)** [4 marks]

- **Drain or pour away stagnant water** and fill up the hollows where water collects.
- **Cover all water storage containers** (barrels, pots, tanks) so that mosquitoes cannot lay eggs in them.
- **Clear the bushes and weeds** around the house, and **cut the grass** short.
- **Spray the rooms with insecticide** (or use mosquito coils) and fix **wire mesh** on the windows.
- **Sleep under a treated mosquito net**, and visit the hospital early when you feel feverish.
- Put **fishes (such as tilapia)** in ponds and wells to eat the larvae.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any FOUR correct methods (4 marks). Accept any practical method really used in Ghana. Do NOT accept ‘praying’, ‘drinking herbal medicine’ or ‘taking malaria drugs regularly’ as control methods - the last one is treatment, not control.*

**(c)** [2 marks]

- Certain **fishes (for example tilapia) are put into the pond** and they **feed on the mosquito larvae and pupae** in the water (1 mark).
- This reduces the number of larvae that develop into adult mosquitoes, so fewer mosquitoes bite people (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each. The method is called **biological control**; accept the term with the correct explanation.*

**(d)** [2 marks]

- Prevention is **cheaper and safer** than cure - treating malaria costs money and the illness can become serious, causing **death or long absence from work and school** (1 mark).
- When mosquitoes are controlled, **the whole family stays healthy**, so no money or time is lost; the measures (net, clearing water, spraying) protect everyone, not only the sick person (1 mark).
  - *Marking note: 2 marks, 1 mark each for any two sound points. Accept ‘prevention is better than cure because you do not fall sick at all’ if supported with a reason such as cost, time lost or danger of death.*

### Question 6 (11 marks)

**(a)** [3 marks]

- **Walk round** the school compound and the nearby houses **looking for water that is standing still** (drains, pits, open barrels, wells, old tyres, bottles, broken pots).
- **Check each place carefully** (using a spoon or a small net) to see whether there are **wrigglers (larvae) or pupae** in the water.
- **Record the findings** in a table or a map of the compound: where the water is, what container holds it, and whether young mosquitoes were found.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any THREE sensible steps (3 marks). This is a design task, so award method marks for any practical step that would really find breeding sites. Look for searching for standing water, confirming the presence of the young stages, and recording.*

**(b)** [3 marks]

- **Collect eggs or wrigglers** from a pond or gutter with a little water, put them in a **glass jar** and cover the jar with a piece of cloth (to keep the adults in).
- **Observe the jar every day**, and **draw and label** each stage as it appears (egg, larva, pupa, adult), recording the changes in a table with dates.
- Alternatively, **draw and label the four stages on a chart** and arrange them in a circle with arrows to show the cycle, and present it to the class.
  - *Marking note: 3 marks: 1 mark for the breeding/observation method, 1 mark for drawing and labelling each stage, 1 mark for recording or presenting the cycle in order. Accept a correctly labelled flow chart as evidence.*

**(c)** [3 marks]

- A **drawing of the four stages** of the life cycle with their names.
- The **breeding places** (stagnant water, open barrels, old tyres, drains) shown crossed out or marked with a big X.
- The **control methods** in pictures: pouring away water, covering containers, sleeping under a net, spraying, clearing bushes.
- A **short clear message and a title** (for example: “No stagnant water, no mosquitoes, no malaria!”).
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any THREE sensible features (3 marks). This is a design task, so award method marks for any feature that would really teach someone how to control mosquitoes.*

**(d)** [2 marks]

- **Cover the breeding jar always** so that adult mosquitoes cannot escape and bite the class.
- **Wash hands with soap after handling pond water** or the young stages, and **do not drink or taste** the water; wear a long-sleeved shirt when going round the compound.
  - *Marking note: 1 mark each for any TWO sensible precautions (2 marks). The key idea is protection from bites and from dirty water. Do NOT accept ‘do not fall in the water’ on its own.*
