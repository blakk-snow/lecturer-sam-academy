# Demonstrate understanding of likelihood of a single outcome occurring by providing examples of events that are impossible, possible, or certain from personal contexts.

## Objectives
- Demonstrate understanding of likelihood of a single outcome occurring by providing examples of events that are impossible, possible, or certain from personal contexts.

## Explanation

*Content standard B7.4.2.1* — Identify the sample space for a probability experiment involving single events and express the probabilities of given events as fractions, decimals, percentages and/or ratios to solve problems
Demonstrate understanding of likelihood of a single outcome occurring by providing examples of events that are impossible, possible, or certain from personal contexts.

## Worked Example
1. Describe each outcome using words like: impossible, possible, or certain. i. The dog will fly tomorrow (impossible). ii. Someone in the class would be a teacher in the future (possible). iii. Ghana will still be an African country tomorrow (certain). E.g. 2. Ask learners to work in groups to discuss the outcome of the following events using words like: impossible, possible, or certain A. A coin lands heads side up. B. The day after Monday will be Tuesday. C. A new-born baby will be a girl. D. It will rain in Winneba in the first week of January.
