# Create and solve story problems involving decimals on the four basic operations.

## Objectives
- Create and solve story problems involving decimals on the four basic operations.

## Explanation

*Content standard B7.1.2.2* — Demonstrate an understanding of addition, subtraction, multiplication and division of (i) whole numbers, and (ii) decimal numbers, to solve problems.
Create and solve story problems involving decimals on the four basic operations.

## Worked Example
1. Solve word problems. (i) A group of two hundred and fifteen men and seven hundred and eighty-four women went to watch a musical concert. An amount of GH₵25 was collected at the gate from each person. How much money was collected all together? (ii) Mrs Adamu bought 13.6kg of meat. Mrs Anderson bought 2.4kg of meat less than Mrs Adamu. How many kilograms of meat did they buy all together? (iii) Ebo weighs 28.6kg. His father weighs four times as heavy. What is the total weight of Ebo and his father? (iv) Mrs Armah bought 45.75 metres of linen for her five children. If they share the material equally, how many metres of linen did each receive? E.g.2 Solve word problems on data presented in a table (i) In preparation towards an open day anniversary, a school’s Management Committee approved the following budget on some projects. Activity Cost (GH₵) Painting school building 4,580 Mending cracks on the basketball pitch 3,050 Restock the library with new books 2,690 Buying of choir robes 5,340 Buying prizes for awards 4,270 (a) How much was approved for painting the school building and buying choir robes? (b) How much more was to be spent on mending the cracks on the basketball pitch than restocking the library with new books? (c) How much was spent on buying prizes for awards if twice the amount approved was spent on this activity?
