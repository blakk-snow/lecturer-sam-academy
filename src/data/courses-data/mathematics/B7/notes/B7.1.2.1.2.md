## Worked Example
1. Apply the halving and doubling techniques to determine the product of two given numbers. i. 28 x 5, think 14 x 10=140 ii. 125 x 4, think (125 x 2) x 2 = 250 x 2 = 500 E.g. 2. Apply the distributive property to determine the product of two given numbers i. 7 x 15, think 7 × (10 + 5) = 70 + 35 = 105 ii. 18 × 6, think (20 − 2) × 6 = (20 × 6)–(6 × 2) = 120 −12 = 108
