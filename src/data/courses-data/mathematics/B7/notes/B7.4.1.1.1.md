# Select and justify a method to collect data (quantitative and qualitative) to answer a given question.

## Objectives
- Select and justify a method to collect data (quantitative and qualitative) to answer a given question.

## Explanation

*Content standard B7.4.1.1* — Select, justify, and use appropriate methods to collect data (quantitative and qualitative), display and analyse the data (grouped/ungrouped) presented in frequency tables, line graphs, pie graphs, bar graphs or pictographs and use these to solve and/or pose problems.
Select and justify a method to collect data (quantitative and qualitative) to answer a given question.

## Worked Example
1. In small groups, learners discuss and write down how they would make decisions in the following situations, what facts they would take into account and how they would collect these ‘facts: (a) The type of drinks to buy for a class party. (b) The make of football boots to buy for the school team. (c) Do people who eat more fufu develop pot belly? (d) The number of desks in each classroom.
