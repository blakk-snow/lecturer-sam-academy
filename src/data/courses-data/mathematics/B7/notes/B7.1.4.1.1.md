# Find ratio and use ratio language to describe relationship between two quantities.

## Objectives
- Find ratio and use ratio language to describe relationship between two quantities.

## Explanation

*Content standard B7.1.4.1* — Demonstrate an understanding of the concept of ratios and its relationship to fractions and use it to solve problems that involve rates, ratios, and proportional reasoning
Find ratio and use ratio language to describe relationship between two quantities.

## Worked Example
1Determine ratio of given quantities. i. There are 60 boys and 120 girls in a school. So the ratio of boys to girls in the school is = 1:2) 60 1 120= 2 E.g.2 Express two quantities as a ratio. i. The ratio of wings to beaks in the bird house at the Kumasi Zoo is 2:1, because for every 2 wings there is 1 beak. E.g.3 Describe quantities with ratio language. i. The ratio of Musa to Alhasan’s age is 1:2. If Alhasan is 50 years old and his son, Musa is 25 years old, we can say that Alhasan is twice as old as his son. Musa is half the age of his father.
