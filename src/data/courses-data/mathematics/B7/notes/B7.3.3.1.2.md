*Content standard B7.3.3.1* — Perform a single transformation (i.e. reflection and translation) on a 2D shape using graph paper (including technology) and describe the properties of the image under the transformation (i.e. congruence, similarity, etc.)
Plot points and shapes (i.e. plane figures) on a coordinate plane and draw their images under reflection in given lines

If (a, b) is reflected on the x-axis, its image is the point (a, -b) ii. If (a, b) is reflected on the y-axis, its image is the point (-a, b) iii. If (a, b) is reflected on the line y = x, its image is the point (b, a) iv. If (a, b) is reflected on the line y = -x, its image is the point (-b, - a) NB: Reflection can occur over a line and/ or in a point.
