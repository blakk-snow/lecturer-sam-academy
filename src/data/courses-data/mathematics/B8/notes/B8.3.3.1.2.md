# Draw rotation image in a coordinate plane and determine the angle of rotation.

## Objectives
- Draw rotation image in a coordinate plane and determine the angle of rotation.

## Explanation

*Content standard B8.3.3.1* — Perform a single transformation (i.e. rotation) on a 2D shape using graph paper (including technology) and describe the properties of the image under the transformation (i.e. congruence)
Draw rotation image in a coordinate plane and determine the angle of rotation.

## Worked Example
1. Rotate a shape through a given centre of rotation and angle of rotation using rotation rules. i. State the object points and its corresponding image points under a given rotation. ii. Draw points of shapes under a clockwise or anti-clockwise rotation through a given angle about the origin (90º, 180º, 270º). E.g. 2. Determine the angle of rotation using the points of an object, its images and centre.
