# Investigate the concept of congruent shapes.

## Objectives
- Investigate the concept of congruent shapes.

## Explanation

*Content standard B8.3.3.1* — Perform a single transformation (i.e. rotation) on a 2D shape using graph paper (including technology) and describe the properties of the image under the transformation (i.e. congruence)
Investigate the concept of congruent shapes.

## Worked Example
1. Use multiple and varied examples of rotation on coordinate planes to verify congruent shapes based on their properties.
