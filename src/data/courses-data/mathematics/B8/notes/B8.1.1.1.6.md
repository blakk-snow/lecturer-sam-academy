# Create and solve word or real-life problems on place values

## Objectives
- Create and solve word or real-life problems on place values

## Explanation

*Content standard B8.1.1.1* — Demonstrate understanding and the use of place value for expressing quantities in standard form and rounding numbers and decimals to significant figures and a given number of decimal places
Create and solve word or real-life problems on place values

## Worked Example
1 Solve word or story problems. (i) Adom earns Gh₵2500 a month after tax and his elder brother Arko earns three times as much. How much is their total income after five years if there are no increases in their earnings?
