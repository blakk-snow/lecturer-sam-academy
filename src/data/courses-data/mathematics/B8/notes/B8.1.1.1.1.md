# Apply the understanding of place value to read and write in number quantities over 1,000,000,000.

## Objectives
- Apply the understanding of place value to read and write in number quantities over 1,000,000,000.

## Explanation

*Content standard B8.1.1.1* — Demonstrate understanding and the use of place value for expressing quantities in standard form and rounding numbers and decimals to significant figures and a given number of decimal places
Apply the understanding of place value to read and write in number quantities over 1,000,000,000.

## Worked Example
1. Read and write numbers in words and vice versa. (i) 2408321: Two million, four hundred and eight thousand, three hundred and twenty-one. (ii) the numeral part of the serial number on a currency note TD1567451, i.e., 1567451: One million, five hundred and sixty- seven thousand, four hundred and fifty-one.
