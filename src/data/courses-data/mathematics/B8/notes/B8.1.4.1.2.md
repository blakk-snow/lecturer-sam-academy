We can divide the number of hours by 3 to get to 1 hour. 756 3 ÷3 ÷ 3 ? 1 756 ÷ 3 = 252 Calories burned hours 756 3 ÷3 ÷ 3 252 1 Salamatu burns 252 calories per hour of drumming.
