# Use the concept of sets to identify perfect squares and determine the square roots.

## Objectives
- Use the concept of sets to identify perfect squares and determine the square roots.

## Explanation

*Content standard B8.1.1.2* — Apply the concepts and vocabulary of sets on sets of factors of numbers to identify perfect squares, determine their square root and solve real life problems involving union and intersection of two sets
Use the concept of sets to identify perfect squares and determine the square roots. Use the knowledge on sets and sets of factors of numbers to solve problems

E.g. 2. Use the knowledge on odd numbers to determine the square root of perfect numbers. (i) Determine the square root of 49.
