# Skip count forwards and backwards in 10,000s, 100,000s, 500,000s, etc.

## Objectives
- Skip count forwards and backwards in 10,000s, 100,000s, 500,000s, etc.

## Explanation

*Content standard B8.1.1.1* — Demonstrate understanding and the use of place value for expressing quantities in standard form and rounding numbers and decimals to significant figures and a given number of decimal places
Skip count forwards and backwards in 10,000s, 100,000s, 500,000s, etc.

## Worked Example
1 Count forward in 500000s up to the fifth number. (i) 200,000, 700,000, ... E.g. 2. Count backwards in 100,500s up to the fifth number. (I) 1,800,000, 1699500, 1599000, ...
