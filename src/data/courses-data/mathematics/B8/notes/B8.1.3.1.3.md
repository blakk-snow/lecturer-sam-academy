# Review word problems involving basic operations on fractions and related concepts.

## Objectives
- Review word problems involving basic operations on fractions and related concepts.

## Explanation

*Content standard B8.1.3.1* — Apply the understanding of operation on fractions to solve problems involving fractions of given quantities and round the results to given decimal and significant places
Review word problems involving basic operations on fractions and related concepts.

## Worked Example
1. Solve word problems involving fractions. i. Determine the (i) perimeter and (ii) area of a rectangle whose sides measure cm by 3 cm. 1 3 ii. Faako answers 42 out of 60 questions correctly. What percentage of her answers are correct? iii. In a school of the students eat from the school feeding programme, bring their packed lunch, and the rest go home to eat. What fraction of the students go home for lunch? iv. Esi and Fusena prepared an orange drink by mixing orange squash and water. Esi’s drink was made of orange squash and Fusena’s was made up of orange squash. Whose drink tastes stronger of orange?
