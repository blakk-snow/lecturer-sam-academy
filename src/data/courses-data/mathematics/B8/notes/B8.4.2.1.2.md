# Express the probabilities of the events as fractions, decimals, percentages and/or ratios.

## Objectives
- Express the probabilities of the events as fractions, decimals, percentages and/or ratios.

## Explanation

*Content standard B8.4.2.1* — Identify the sample space for a probability experiment involving two independent events and express the probabilities of given events as fractions, decimals, percentages and/or ratios to solve problems.
Express the probabilities of the events as fractions, decimals, percentages and/or ratios.
