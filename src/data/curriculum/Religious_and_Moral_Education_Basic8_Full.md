Describe the naming ceremonies/outdooring in the three major religions Islam A child is given a name on the eighth day. A ceremony made up of couple’s relatives, community members and led by an imam or a mallam. A sheep is slaughtered to thank Allah for the health of the mother and child. Explain physical and emotional changes that characterise puberty

**Exemplars:**

1. Explain the meaning of puberty • It is the transition from childhood to adulthood. It occurs between the ages of 10 to 19 (WHO).
2. Discuss physical and emotional changes that occur at puberty Physical changes in girls at puberty: • Growing of pubic hair • Menstruation • Breasts develop and increase in size • Hips widen • Change in height, etc. Physical changes in boys at puberty: • Pubic hair • Break in voice • Sperms development • Penis grows bigger • Change in height etc. Emotional changes in both sexes: • Curiosity • Adventurous • Conflict of identity (not child, not adult) • Mood swings • Development of the urge to have sex, etc. Learners to write their concerns relating to puberty anonymously and place them in a box. The teacher discusses these concerns in class to address any fears or misconceptions about puberty.
