#### Indicator B8/JHS2.4.3.1.1  *(printed in the curriculum as “B8.4.3.1.1”)*

Describe the principles underlying the operation of the components of a robot (Controller Mechanical, Sensors) Exemplar(s): 1. Explain the controller as the “brain” of the robot. 2.
