# STRAND 1: B9.1 CUSTOMS AND INSTITUTIONS

## SUB-STRAND 1: B9.1.1 RITES OF PASSAGE: MARRIAGE

*Basic 9 (JHS 3) — curriculum pages 84 of the PDF.*

### Content Standard B9/JHS3.1.1.1

**Demonstrate an understanding of marriage of their culture and compare with other cultures of Ghana**

#### Indicator B9/JHS3.1.1.1.1  *(printed in the curriculum as “B9.1.1.1.1”)*

Discuss the processes involved in performing marriage rites in their culture.

**Exemplars:**

1. Explain what marriage is.
2. Read and discuss a text on marriage.
3. Identify types of marriage.
4. Identify some of the marriage rites in their culture and write them.
5. Discuss marriage rites performance.
6. Explore the significance of the processes involved in performing marriage rites.

**Core competencies:** Communication and Collaboration (CC); Cultural Identity and Global Citizenship (CG)

- CC 7.3: Provide feedback in areas of ideas, organisation, voice, word choice and sentence fluency in communication.
- CG 5.4: Develop and exhibit the sense of cultural identity.

#### Indicator B9/JHS3.1.1.1.2  *(printed in the curriculum as “B9.1.1.1.2”)*

Compare and contrast the marriage rites in their culture to other cultures in Ghana.

**Exemplars:**

1. Present marriage rites among their people.
2. Relate the marriage rites of their culture to those of other Ghanaian cultures.

**Core competencies:** Communication and Collaboration (CC); Cultural Identity and Global Citizenship (CG)

- CC 7.3: Provide feedback in areas of ideas, organisation, voice, word choice and sentence fluency in communication.
- CG 5.5: Adjust to the demands of customs, traditions, values and attitudes of society.

#### Indicator B9/JHS3.1.1.1.3  *(printed in the curriculum as “B9.1.1.1.3”)*

Compare and contrast the traditional marriage rites to the contemporary marriage rites and dramatise both.

**Exemplars:**

1. Explain some of the contemporary marriage rites in their culture.
2. Compare and contrast the traditional to the contemporary marriage rites.
3. Dramatise marriage rites session of their culture.

**Core competencies:** Communication and Collaboration (CC); Cultural Identity and Global Citizenship (CG)

- CC 7.3: Provide feedback in areas of ideas, organisation, voice, word choice and sentence fluency in communication.
- CG 5.5: Adjust to the demands of customs, traditions, values and attitudes of society.

### Content Standard B9/JHS3.6.1.2

#### Indicator B9/JHS3.6.1.2.1  *(printed in the curriculum as “B9.6.1.2.1”)*

**Exemplars:**
