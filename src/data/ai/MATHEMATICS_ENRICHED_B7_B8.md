# MATHEMATICS — ENRICHED SCHEME OF LEARNING
## Basic 7 and Basic 8 · All weeks · All three terms

The week-by-week scheme from `BASIC 7 SCHEME OF LEARNING.pdf` / `BASIC 8 SCHEME OF LEARNING.pdf` (Beacon Educational Consult, 2026/2027), joined to the NaCCA Mathematics Common Core Programme curriculum for JHS 1–3 (September 2020) from `maths_ccp_2023.pdf`.

Each week gives the scheme's strand, sub-strand and resources, then — for every indicator the scheme cites — the curriculum's content standard wording, the indicator wording with its worked examples, and the core competencies.

**Indicator matching:** the scheme cites 124 indicator references (ranges such as `B7.1.1.1.1-2` expanded). **120 matched** the curriculum. **4 codes the scheme cites do not exist in the NaCCA curriculum** and are flagged in place: B7.1.1.1.2, B7.1.2.2.2, B7.3.1.2.5, B7.3.1.2.6. (Note that B7.3.1.2 runs .1, .2, .3, .7 in the curriculum — .4, .5 and .6 were never issued.)

**Two caveats on the curriculum text.** First, the source PDF prints some codes with overlapping text runs (e.g. `B8.2.2.1.2` on p. 148 renders unreadably); those two entries — B7.4.2.1.3 and B8.2.2.1.2 — were recovered from `nacca_maths_fulltext.txt` and carry no core-competency list. Second, worked examples that the curriculum lays out as two-dimensional arithmetic (column addition, fraction bars, expansion grids) read as jumbled characters once flattened to a line. **Use the wording and the competency lists as authoritative, but open `maths_ccp_2023.pdf` for any worked example you intend to put in front of a class.**
